north indian food

North Indian cuisine is a rich, diverse culinary tradition characterized by creamy, slow-cooked gravies, extensive use of dairy (ghee, butter, paneer), and wheat-based breads like naan and roti.

about south indian food

South Indian cuisine is a 4,000-year-old culinary tradition defined by rice, lentils, coconut, tamarind, and curry leaves, producing bold, tangy, and earthy flavors.
